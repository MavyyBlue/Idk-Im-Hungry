# Product Rules

1. Never confront the user with a large food menu during the decision flow.
2. One dominant question/candidate at a time; the results screen may show approximately three survivors.
3. The unusual reaction vocabulary must remain visible through slider mood states, especially Shrug, Ehhh, and Nnngh.
4. Every question starts neutral at Ehhh; the interface must not pre-answer for the user.
5. Below-middle slider answers must materially lower matching-food scores.
6. Negative evidence must be more decisive than symmetric positive evidence.
7. The far-left Absolutely Not zone is authoritative for the current session.
8. Shortlist Nope is a hard exact-item rejection: that food becomes zero-viability and cannot return in the session.
9. Nope replaces only the rejected shortlist slot; untouched suggestions must not reshuffle unnecessarily.
10. Hidden Safe Foods are excluded completely until unhidden.
11. Safe Food means known viable option, not must-recommend. Current-session answers can still eliminate it.
12. Safe Food keywords should be tap-first chips, not a typing chore.
13. If restaurant suggestions stall, change questioning strategy.
14. Time of day is an opening context signal, never a rule forbidding off-hours cravings.
15. Keep Drilling must produce a genuine refinement round rather than replaying the same top three.
16. Exact answered questions must not repeat; repeatedly adverse dimensions should lose question priority.
17. Questions should become more niche only when the remaining branch makes them relevant.
18. Compatibility indicators should be allowed to look weak; do not inflate low matches to seem nicer.
19. Keep player-facing UI free of developer explanations and implementation notes.
20. Keep the tone comforting and playful, not diagnostic or medicalized.
21. Preserve working functionality during refactors.
22. Literal Unsafe Foods are authoritative account-level exclusions and override Safe Foods/history.
23. Never describe Literal Unsafe Foods as certified allergy, ingredient, or cross-contact safety.
24. A larger catalog must lead to more specific questioning, not a longer generic interrogation.
25. Product decisions are collaborative with Mavyy; do not genericize the app's personality.
