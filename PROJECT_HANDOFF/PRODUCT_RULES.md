# Product Rules

1. Never confront the user with a large menu.
2. One dominant question/candidate at a time; result screen may show approximately three survivors.
3. Shrug, Ehhh, and Nnngh are meaningful and distinct.
4. No means no. Do not let personalization overrule explicit rejection.
5. If restaurant suggestions stall, change questioning strategy.
6. Use rejection as a decision aid; “Which sounds least bad?” is a first-class prompt.
7. Keep the tone comforting and playful, not diagnostic or medicalized.
8. Make the core loop excellent before adding infrastructure.
9. Preserve working functionality during refactors.
10. Product decisions are collaborative with Mavyy; do not genericize the app’s unusual reaction vocabulary.
