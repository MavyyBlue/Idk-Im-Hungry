# Product Rules

1. Never confront the user with a large food menu during the decision flow.
2. One dominant question/candidate at a time; result screen may show approximately three survivors.
3. Shrug, Ehhh, and Nnngh are meaningful and distinct.
4. No means no. Do not let personalization overrule explicit rejection.
5. Hidden Safe Foods are excluded completely until the user unhides them.
6. Safe Food means “known viable option,” not “must recommend.” Current-session answers can eliminate it.
7. If restaurant suggestions stall, change questioning strategy.
8. Time of day is an opening context signal, never a rule forbidding off-hours cravings.
9. Keep Drilling must produce a genuine refinement round; it must not simply replay the same top three because they have historical score momentum.
10. Nope on a shortlist item is a hard session rejection; that exact item cannot reappear in that session.
11. Use rejection as a decision aid; “Which sounds least bad?” is a first-class prompt.
12. Questions should become more niche only when the remaining branch makes them relevant.
13. Keep the tone comforting and playful, not diagnostic or medicalized.
14. Make the core loop excellent before adding infrastructure.
15. Preserve working functionality during refactors.
16. Product decisions are collaborative with Mavyy; do not genericize the app’s unusual reaction vocabulary.
